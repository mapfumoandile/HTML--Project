WHAT IS ONLINE STREAMING?

Streaming refers to any media content – live or recorded – delivered to computers and mobile devices via the internet and played back in real time. Podcasts, webcasts, movies, TV shows and music videos are common forms of streaming content. There are various platform that provide streaming content such as YouTube, Twitch, Spotify Amazon Prime, etc.
The impact Online Streaming has on the people that consume it is very niche and it explains the gaps the industry still needs to bridge in order to make it convenient for all consumers.
Based on our research we have found out that the most used platforms used for Online Streaming are YouTube and Spotify and that is due to it’s catering plans such as a
Free Plan which allows users to be able to use the platform for free but with a few compromises and a Premium Plan where the compromises are addressed.
Online Streaming has taken over the modern era of entertainment and it proves to be a one of the prominent and rising industry globally today.
